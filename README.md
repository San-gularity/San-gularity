### `whoami`

Software engineer working across full-stack, distributed systems and applied ML. Currently doing my **M.S. in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Chicago** (GPA 4.0), and building internal platforms for **65,000+ campus members** at UIC Student Affairs Technology.

Most recently a **Software Developer Intern at RadioFX Inc.** in Chicago, where I modernized five-year-old microservices, automated Android app creation end to end through a Slack bot, and cut a repository's clone size from **15 GB to 700 MB** while shipping updates to the Play Store.

Before that: **TopRankers** (React Native app modernization, an ML tagging pipeline over 100K+ questions) and **DRDO** (CNN/RNN architectures for radar pulse de-interleaving).
